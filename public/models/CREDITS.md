# Bundled 3D assets — attribution

These assets ship as demonstration content for the Atlas 3D workspace. Replace
them with your own project models (GLB/GLTF, or IFC exported to GLB).

## littlest-tokyo.glb
"Littlest Tokyo" by **Glen Fox** — licensed **CC-BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://artstation.com/artwork/1AGwX (distributed with three.js examples).
Used here unmodified as a reference asset to demonstrate real GLB rendering.

## /hdri/venice_sunset_1k.hdr
"Venice Sunset" environment map — **CC0** (public domain), via Poly Haven /
HDRI Haven (distributed with three.js examples). Used for image-based lighting.

## /draco/
